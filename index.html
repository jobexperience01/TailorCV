Career & Professional Timeline

Your career and education milestones, shown on the resume as a boxed three-column vertical timeline — most recent milestone at the top of column 1, reading down each column then across to the next, with NOW and CAREER START markers so the direction is always clear. One milestone is selected by default below; rename or add more with "+ Add item", or add an empty one and let the ATS Tailoring step further down fill it in from the rest of your resume when you tailor with Claude. It flows onto page 2+ automatically if it does not fit on page 1.

Additional Sections

Add extra sections like Hobbies, Volunteer Work, Online Certifications, Languages, etc. Pick a type (or Custom) then click Add — you can rename it, list its items, and reorder it with the other sections above. Each type shows on the resume in its own presentable style (tag cloud, proficiency table, contact cards, dated entries…), and any date box is optional — fill it in only if you want it shown. It flows onto page 2+ automatically if it does not fit on page 1, so there is nothing to set manually for that.

Tailor to a Job (ATS)

ATS Tailoring
Choose No limit to skip the page limit and word count entirely. Otherwise the generated prompt tells Claude exactly how many lines and roughly how many words fit on this template's page for the selected paper size (measured from the actual page margins and font), and asks it to fit the tailored resume within your chosen number of pages — trimming the least relevant content first rather than shrinking text or margins.
The percentage is how aggressively the resume is tailored to the target job. It is not a claim that the resume matches that share of the job description.
